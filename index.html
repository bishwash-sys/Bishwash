Welcome to Shree Navajagriti chandi secondary school ! In an era defined by rapid digital transformation, education must evolve. At our institution, we believe that being 'future-ready' means more than just academic excellence; it means mastering the digital languages that shape our world. Our mission is to empower the next generation of Digital Leaders. Whether our students are diving into the complexities of Computer Organization, mastering Java and C programming, or exploring Physics and Mathematics, we provide an environment where theory meets practice. We don't just teach students how to use technology—we challenge them to create it. As we follow the NEB curriculum, we integrate hands-on coding and computational thinking into our daily learning. We are proud to cultivate a community of innovators who are prepared to lead Nepal's digital future with integrity and skill. I invite you to join us on this journey of discovery and innovation. Together, let’s code the future together.

--Thakur Prasad pandey Principle,Shree Navajagriti chandi secondary school
